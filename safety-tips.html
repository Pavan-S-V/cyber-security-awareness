🛡️ PERSONAL CYBERSECURITY GUIDE

Simple habits. Stronger protection.

Most online scams succeed because attackers create urgency, fear, curiosity or trust. Learn simple habits that can help you recognize suspicious activity before you take action.

THE GOLDEN RULES

Remember these 5 rules.

These simple habits can protect you from many common online scams.

01
⏸️

Stop

Do not act immediately when a message creates panic, fear or urgency.

02
🔎

Check

Examine the sender, link, request and other warning signs carefully.

03
📞

Verify

Contact the organization using an official website or trusted contact method.

04
🔐

Protect

Never reveal passwords, OTPs, PINs or sensitive banking information.

05
📢

Report

If something appears fraudulent, report it through the appropriate official channel.

PROTECTION CHECKLIST

Protect every part of your digital life.

Follow these practical security habits regularly.

🔐

Passwords & Accounts

  • ✓ Use strong and unique passwords.
  • ✓ Enable multi-factor authentication where available.
  • ✓ Never share your password with anyone.
  • ✓ Avoid using the same password everywhere.
🔑

OTP & PIN Safety

  • ✓ Never share an OTP with callers.
  • ✓ Banks and legitimate services should not ask you to disclose your OTP.
  • ✓ Protect your UPI PIN.
  • ✓ Never enter a PIN just because someone asks.
🔗

Links & Messages

  • ✓ Check unexpected links before opening them.
  • ✓ Be cautious with shortened URLs.
  • ✓ Watch for spelling and domain-name tricks.
  • ✓ Do not trust urgency alone.
📱

Mobile Applications

  • ✓ Install apps from trusted sources.
  • ✓ Review permissions before allowing access.
  • ✓ Avoid unknown APK files.
  • ✓ Keep your device and apps updated.
💳

Banking & Payments

  • ✓ Verify payment requests independently.
  • ✓ Never transfer money because of threats.
  • ✓ Check the recipient before paying.
  • ✓ Do not scan unknown QR codes.
👤

Personal Information

  • ✓ Limit sensitive information shared online.
  • ✓ Be careful with unknown callers.
  • ✓ Review privacy settings regularly.
  • ✓ Think before posting personal details.
🚨 IF SOMEONE THREATENS YOU

Digital arrest? Stay calm.

Fraudsters may pretend to be police officers, investigators or government officials. They may use fear and urgency to demand money or sensitive information.

01 Stay calm and do not obey threats immediately.
02 Do not transfer money or reveal OTPs/PINs.
03 End the call and independently verify the claim.
04 Report suspected fraud through an appropriate official channel.
🚫
Remember Fear is a scammer's weapon.
BEFORE YOU CLICK

Ask yourself these questions.

01

Do I know the sender?

If not, treat the message carefully.

02

Is this request unexpected?

Unexpected requests deserve independent verification.

03

Am I being pressured?

Urgency and threats are common manipulation techniques.

04

Is sensitive information requested?

Never casually share credentials, OTPs or financial details.

🆘
IF YOU THINK YOU HAVE BEEN SCAMMED

Act quickly. Don't hide it.

Stop further communication, secure affected accounts, contact the relevant financial service if money was involved, and report the incident through an appropriate official channel.
