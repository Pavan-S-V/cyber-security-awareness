THREAT TYPE 01

Phishing Scams

Phishing uses fake messages, websites or links to trick people into revealing sensitive information or taking unsafe actions.

⚠️

Warning Signs

  • ! Unexpected messages asking you to act immediately.
  • ! Requests for OTPs, passwords or verification codes.
  • ! Links asking you to log in or verify an account.
  • ! Threats that your account will be blocked.
SCAM EXAMPLE SMS
B
BANK ALERT Unknown sender

⚠️ URGENT! Your account will be suspended today. Verify your KYC immediately using the link below.

http://example-security-login.com
⚠️ This is a suspicious pattern.
🛡️
What should you do?

Verify the request through the organization's official website or app. Never trust the link inside an unexpected message.

THREAT TYPE 02

Digital Arrest Scams

Criminals may impersonate police, government officials or investigators and use fear, threats and urgency to manipulate victims.

🚨

Common Warning Signs

  • ! Someone claims you are involved in a crime.
  • ! Threats of immediate arrest or legal action.
  • ! Pressure to stay on a video or phone call.
  • ! Demands for money to "clear" or "close" a case.
SCAM EXAMPLE VIDEO CALL
👮
"CYBER CRIME DEPARTMENT"

Your identity has been linked to an illegal transaction. Transfer money immediately for verification or an arrest warrant will be issued.

⚠️ Threats + urgency + money demand
🛑
Remember

Do not transfer money because someone threatens you with arrest. Stop the call and independently verify the claim.

📱
THREAT TYPE 03

Malicious Applications

Unsafe applications can be used to steal information, abuse permissions or provide attackers with access to a device.

📱

Red Flags

  • ! APK files received from unknown sources.
  • ! Apps asking for unnecessary permissions.
  • ! Someone asks you to install an app during a call.
  • ! Apps claiming to provide suspicious rewards or benefits.

Stay safer

Install applications only from trusted sources and review permissions before granting access.

PROTECTION CENTER

Your Cyber Safety Checklist

Simple habits can prevent many common digital scams.

01
🔑

Protect Credentials

Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs or verification codes with unexpected callers or messages.

02
🔗

Check Links

Be cautious with unexpected links. Open the official website or app yourself instead of using a suspicious link.

03
⏸️

Stop Under Pressure

Urgency, fear and excitement are common manipulation techniques. Pause and verify.

04
🏦

Verify Independently

Contact the organization using an official number or website, not the details supplied in the suspicious message.

05
📱

Review Apps

Avoid unknown applications and review permissions before granting access.

06
🛑

Don't Rush

If a message demands immediate action, take a moment to verify it before responding.
