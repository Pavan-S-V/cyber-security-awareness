ABOUT CYBERSAFE

Helping People Stay Safe Online.

CyberSafe is a cybersecurity awareness platform created to help users recognize, understand, and respond to common digital threats.

OUR PLATFORM

What is CyberSafe?

CyberSafe is designed to make cybersecurity easier to understand for everyday users. The platform focuses on common threats such as phishing, digital arrest scams, malicious applications, suspicious messages, and deceptive URLs.

Instead of simply showing a technical warning, CyberSafe provides understandable explanations and practical recommendations so users can make safer decisions before clicking, sharing information, or making payments.
